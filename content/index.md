**ComAtlas** is a source-traceable knowledge graph of the **NVIDIA supply chain** — every
relationship is backed by a short quote from a public filing and a link to its SEC source.

Start with the [interactive graph](/graph/), or open a company page (e.g. [[nvidia|NVIDIA]],
[[tsmc|TSMC]], [[mellanox|Mellanox]]) to read its profile, its supply / competition / partnership
relationships, and reported financials.

**How to read a page:** the summary at the top is machine-generated from cited filings; the
relations table lists each edge with its ≤15-word evidence excerpt and a link to the source
document; financial facts come from XBRL. "medium" edges are single-source and not yet
cross-verified.

[Disclaimer](/disclaimer) — ComAtlas presents source-traceable public facts, **not investment advice**.
