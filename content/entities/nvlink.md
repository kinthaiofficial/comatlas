<!-- SUMMARY:BEGIN -->
NVLink is NVIDIA's high-speed GPU interconnect; with NVLink switches it lets large numbers of GPUs act as a single data-center-scale computer. The FY2026 10-K notes NVIDIA introduced NVLink Fusion to integrate custom CPUs and XPUs with its platform.
